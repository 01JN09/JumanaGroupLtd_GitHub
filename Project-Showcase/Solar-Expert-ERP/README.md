# ☀️ The Complete Operating System for Solar Businesses

**Transforming the way solar businesses operate.**

SolarExpert ERP Suite is an all-in-one business management platform designed for solar installation companies, renewable energy contractors, and EPC firms.

Instead of relying on multiple spreadsheets, disconnected software, and manual processes, SolarExpert centralizes every department into one intelligent platform—allowing businesses to manage sales, operations, projects, finance, field teams, and customer relationships from a single environment.

Whether managing residential installations or large commercial projects, SolarExpert provides the visibility and control needed to scale operations efficiently.

---

# Why SolarExpert?

Running a successful solar business requires more than completing installations.

It requires coordinating customers, quotations, technicians, inventory, finances, projects, and after-sales support—all while ensuring every department stays synchronized.

SolarExpert was built to simplify these operations, reduce manual work, and give management complete oversight of the business.

---

# Business Capabilities

### Customer Management

Manage enquiries, customer records, quotations, contracts, project history, and communication throughout the customer journey.

### Project Operations

Plan installations, monitor project progress, assign field teams, schedule interventions, and track every active project from initiation to completion.

### Workforce Management

Coordinate technicians, monitor workloads, organize daily schedules, manage interventions, and maintain operational efficiency across multiple teams.

### Inventory Control

Monitor equipment availability, manage suppliers, control stock movement, and ensure projects always have the required materials.

### Finance & Administration

Generate quotations and invoices, track payments, monitor financial performance, and maintain complete operational records.

### Website Management

Update business information, services, company branding, media, and website content directly from the administration portal without relying on developers.
